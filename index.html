Make-up credits

A credit is earned when a regular lesson is cancelled inside your policy or by you (teacher). Booking a make-up lesson uses the student's oldest available credit. If the make-up itself is cancelled inside policy or by you, the credit comes back; a late cancel or no-show on a make-up uses it up — that's the policy most studios run, and it's stated to families up front.

How LessonBook works

  • Roster → Week: set each student's usual day/time, then "Generate week from roster" builds the week's lessons in one tap.
  • Mark every lesson: Taught, Cancelled — in policy (earns a make-up credit), Cancelled — late / no-show (no credit; per-lesson students are still charged, which is the standard policy), or Cancelled by teacher (earns a credit).
  • Credits: expire after the number of days in Settings (default 60). Book make-ups from the Credits tab — the oldest available credit is used first.
  • Money: monthly students are charged with the one-tap tuition button; per-lesson students are charged automatically when a payable lesson is marked. Record payments as they arrive — balances and statements stay current.
  • Statements: open any student and print a statement / term progress report — tuition, payments, balance, lessons, practice notes, credits and milestones on one page.
  • Honest limits: LessonBook records payments — it never charges a card. It doesn't send reminders or sync between devices; backup export/import is the transfer path. One book per device/browser.
